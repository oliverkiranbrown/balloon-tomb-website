## Design Context

### Users
A mix of existing fans, people discovering the band for the first time at a gig, and press/bookers. When they land, they should immediately *get* what Balloon Tomb is — no explanation needed. The site is both a first impression and a home base.

### Brand Personality
**Silly, chaotic, joyous.** A band that yells about goblins, dating apps, and trains with complete commitment and zero irony. The energy is loud and self-aware — this is not a band that takes itself seriously, but it's also not a joke. It's genuinely fun.

### Aesthetic Direction
**8-bit retro video game meets disco-punk ordered-chaos zine.** Think a photocopied gig flyer that's been scanned at too high a resolution — but also like the title screen of a 1987 arcade game you'd find in a sticky Bristol pub. The chaos should feel *intentional*, not accidental.

- **Theme**: Dark. Gigs happen at night. People browse this after a show, on their phone, in a sweaty venue or on the bus home.
- **Colors**: Pink, black, and white strictly. No deviation. Pink (`oklch(0.65 0.28 0)` approx) as the sole accent — used sparingly so it hits hard. Black backgrounds tinted ever so slightly warm. Off-white text, never pure white.
- **Typography**: PressStart2P is the established brand font and must stay as the display/heading face. Body text (for lyrics, manifesto prose, longer passages) uses **VT323** as the second font — a CRT/terminal pixel font that's very readable at larger sizes, feels like an SNES text box, and pairs with PressStart2P without competing. NOT a clean sans.
- **Lyrics pages**: 90s JRPG credits screen aesthetic. Centered text, black void, subtle CRT scanlines. Song titles in PressStart2P/pink with ★ decorations. Lyric lines in VT323 at large size. The joke is presenting absurd content (songs about fish tanks, trains) with the same ceremony as a Final Fantasy ending sequence.
- **Anti-references**: Anything that looks like a SaaS landing page, a Spotify artist profile, or a generic "band website template." No rounded cards with drop shadows. No hero with a centered headline and a CTA button.

### Design Principles
1. **Ordered chaos** — layouts should feel like they broke slightly and nobody fixed them, but in a way that's clearly intentional. Slight rotations, offset elements, asymmetric grids.
2. **Mobile-first, always** — the audience is on phones. Every decision starts from 375px wide.
3. **Pink is precious** — one accent color used with restraint. It should feel like a highlight marker on a photocopied zine page, not a theme color applied everywhere.
4. **Clean bones, rough skin** — underlying structure must be solid (accessible, performant, readable). The roughness is surface-level texture, not structural disorder.
5. **No filler** — every element earns its place. No decorative cards, no repeated patterns just to fill space, no stock-photo energy.
